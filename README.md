## About Me

I am an Informatics Management student at Fakultas Ilmu Komputer, Universitas Sriwijaya, with an interest in web development, UI/UX design, system analysis, and digital product development.

I have experience as a System Analyst Intern at PT Bukit Asam Tbk, where I was involved in system analysis, business requirement documentation, process flow design, and UML-based system modeling. I also enjoy building digital projects that combine functionality, clean structure, and user-centered design.
